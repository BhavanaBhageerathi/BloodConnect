BLOOD DONATION BEFORE, DURING, AND AFTERCARE

Before
- Eat a balanced meal; do not donate while fasting.
- Drink water and arrive hydrated.
- Get adequate sleep.
- Follow the blood centre's instructions about alcohol and other restrictions.
- Bring required identification.
- Tell staff about illness, medicines, allergies, recent procedures, travel, and medical history.

During
- Follow staff instructions.
- Immediately tell staff about dizziness, nausea, sweating, tingling, pain, or feeling unwell.
- Avoid unnecessary movement of the donation arm.

After
- Rest for the period recommended by the blood centre.
- Drink fluids over the next 24–48 hours.
- Eat a balanced meal.
- Avoid strenuous exercise and heavy lifting for several hours or as instructed.
- Keep the bandage on for the advised period.
- If bleeding resumes, apply firm pressure and raise the arm; seek staff advice if it does not stop.
- If light-headed, sit or lie down safely. Do not drive or operate machinery until recovered.
- Contact a healthcare professional for persistent or severe symptoms.

Common mild effects
Mild bruising, soreness, or brief light-headedness can occur. Severe, persistent, or unusual symptoms require medical advice.

Blood-centre aftercare instructions take precedence over this general information.
