BLOODCONNECT APPLICATION GUIDE

Purpose
BloodConnect is a blood donation management platform intended to connect donors, requesters/patients, blood banks, hospitals, and administrators. It supports donation coordination and information sharing.

Important limitation
BloodConnect is a coordination platform. It does not itself collect, test, store, or transfuse blood. These activities are performed by authorized blood centres and healthcare facilities.

User roles
- Donor: creates an account, maintains a donor profile, submits verification information when required, manages donation availability, and may respond to donation requests or participate in camps.
- Requester: creates a blood request and provides information required by the application. For urgent needs, contact the treating hospital and licensed blood centre directly.
- Blood Bank: an eligible/approved account may manage its profile, inventory information, and operations permitted by the application.
- Administrator: oversees users, verification/moderation workflows, records, and platform operations.

Registration and account access
Users register and sign in through the application. Never share passwords, OTPs, access tokens, refresh tokens, or API keys with the chatbot.

Donor profile and verification
A donor may need to provide profile and verification details. Verification status is determined by the application's review workflow. The chatbot cannot approve a donor or change verification status.

Donor availability
Availability is a platform status, not medical clearance. Donors must still be screened by blood-centre staff before donating.

Project cooldown rule
BloodConnect's configured project rule is a cooldown of 3 months for male donors and 4 months for female/other donors after whole-blood donation. Actual eligibility and timing must be confirmed by the blood centre under current rules.

Blood requests
Requesters submit requests using the application's workflow. Inventory and request information may change. Contact the blood centre or hospital directly to confirm availability, component, compatibility, and urgency.

Blood bank inventory
Inventory displayed in BloodConnect is informational and may change. Confirm directly with the blood centre before travelling or making clinical decisions. Compatibility is determined by blood-bank testing.

Camps
Camp registration or availability in the application does not guarantee acceptance to donate. Donors undergo screening at the camp or blood centre.

Privacy
Do not enter unnecessary sensitive medical information, government ID numbers, passwords, OTPs, or payment information into the chatbot. The chatbot must not reveal another user's profile, request, donor details, or conversation.

Troubleshooting
- Sign-in issue: check credentials and use the supported account-recovery process.
- Verification pending: wait for the review process or contact platform support.
- Incorrect availability/cooldown: contact platform support; medical eligibility is decided by the blood centre.
- Urgent request: contact the hospital and licensed blood centre directly.

Chatbot scope
The chatbot explains application features and general blood-donation education. It cannot perform account actions unless a dedicated authenticated API is implemented. It must not make medical decisions and should acknowledge when information is unavailable.
